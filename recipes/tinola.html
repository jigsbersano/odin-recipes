Tinolang Manok

Home

A warm and comforting Filipino chicken soup.

MAhinumdoman nimo imong pagka maayong tao sa kalami

Diri ta sa tinowang manok erp

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Ginger
  • Sayote
  • Cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper

    • How to do this?

    1. Sauté garlic, onion, and ginger.
    2. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
    3. Add water and fish sauce.
    4. Simmer until the chicken is tender.
    5. Add malunggay and serve.
