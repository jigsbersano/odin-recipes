Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Coconut Milk
  • Potato
  • Onion
  • Salt
  • Pepper

    • How to do this?

    1. Sauté garlic and onion.
    2. Add chicken and cook until browned.
    3. Add curry powder and stir.
    4. Add coconut milk and potatoes.
    5. Simmer until cooked.
